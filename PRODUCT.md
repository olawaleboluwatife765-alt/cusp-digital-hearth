# Cusp Product Overview

## Product Vision

Cusp is a next-generation self-custodial Bitcoin and Stacks wallet designed to make digital ownership simple, secure, and intuitive.

Our vision is to remove the complexity that discourages people from entering Web3 while preserving the ownership, transparency, and security that make blockchain technology valuable.

Cusp serves as the transition point between familiar Web2 experiences and true Web3 ownership.

---

# Mission

Empower anyone to confidently own, manage, and interact with digital assets through a wallet experience that feels as familiar as modern consumer applications.

---

# The Problem

Today's wallets often overwhelm new users.

Common pain points include:

- Complex onboarding
- Seed phrase anxiety
- Confusing terminology
- Poor information hierarchy
- Unclear permissions
- Fragmented wallet experiences
- Difficult discovery of trusted applications

Many people abandon Web3 before they complete their first transaction.

---

# Our Solution

Cusp simplifies onboarding without sacrificing self-custody.

The experience combines familiar design patterns with transparent blockchain interactions.

Instead of forcing users to understand everything immediately, Cusp introduces concepts progressively as users become more comfortable.
